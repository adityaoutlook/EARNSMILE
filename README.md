😊 EARN Smiles

EARN Smiles is a fun app that rewards real smiles with USDC (digital money).

How it works

📸 Smile – Take a selfie with a real, natural smile

🤖 AI Check – An AI scores your smile (1–5 ⭐)

💰 Reward – Good smiles (4–5 ⭐) earn USDC

🔗 Blockchain – Your smile + reward are saved securely and can’t be changed

🖼️ Smile Gallery – Best smiles appear in a public gallery

What is USDC?

Digital money

1 USDC ≈ 1 USD

Stored safely in a crypto wallet

Why blockchain?

No cheating

No fake rewards

Full transparency & trust

Goal

Spread happiness 🌈 and reward genuine joy using technology.

In short:
Smile → AI verifies → Blockchain saves → You earn USDC 😄💰
