😊 What is EARNSMILE?

💡 The Magic Idea

Based Smiles is an app that rewards real happiness! If you give a genuine smile, you get digital money (USDC) 💰

📸 Step 1: You Smile

  • Open the app
  • Take a selfie while smiling 😁
  • Show us your real, natural smile!

🤖 Step 2: Robot Checks Your Smile

Our smart AI robot 🤖 analyzes your smile:

  • Are your eyes smiling?
  • Is your smile natural?
  • Are you really happy?

Your smile gets a score from 1 to 5 ⭐

✅ Step 3: Good Smile = Reward

Score 4-5? You win! 🎉

  • Earn 5 USDC (digital money)
  • Build your streak 🔥
  • Unlock badges 🏆

Score below 4? Try again tomorrow! 😊

💰 What is USDC?

  • USDC = 1 US Dollar = digital pocket money 💵
  • Stable value (doesn't change much)
  • Save it in your digital wallet 🐷
  • Trade or transfer it anytime

🔥 Build Your Streak

  • Smile every day to build a streak
  • 3+ days = Bronze Badge 🥉
  • 7+ days = Silver Badge 🥈
  • 30+ days = Gold Legend 🏆

🖼️ Smile Gallery

Your approved smiles go into your personal gallery! See your happy memories and track your journey 📸

🚫 Fair & Secure

  • ❌ You can't use the same photo twice
  • ❌ No fake accounts allowed
  • ✅ Our AI stops cheaters
  • ✅ One real smile per day per person

🎯 Why Based Smiles?

We believe happiness should be rewarded! Technology can spread joy, build community, and prove that computers can be a force for good. 🌈
